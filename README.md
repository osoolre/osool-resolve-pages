# Osool Resolve Pages

Public policy/support pages for **Osool Resolve**, an internal employee application.

Osool Resolve is not a customer-facing app. Authorized Osool employees sign in to follow, manage, and update internal requests, cases, and tickets.

- `/privacy-policy/` — Privacy Policy

## Account model
Authorized employees create their own account in the app. The account remains pending until reviewed and activated by an authorized system administrator.

## Final account lifecycle

1. Authorized employee creates an account in the app.
2. The account remains pending.
3. An authorized system administrator reviews and activates the account.
4. The employee uses Osool Resolve according to assigned permissions.
5. The employee can submit an account deletion request from within the app.
6. An authorized system administrator reviews and confirms the deletion request.
7. The employee account is deleted.
8. Legitimate company business, ticket, case, security, and audit records may be retained where required or permitted.

This workflow is reflected across the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Account & Data Deletion, and Support pages.
